Privacy Policy — Nibblenado Publisher

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Nibblenado Publisher ("the App") is a personal, single-user tool operated by BenLaDefInc. It does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to anyone other than its own developer, and it is not used by any member of the public.

1. Who uses the App

The App has exactly one user: its developer. It is not offered to, or usable by, the general public.

2. What data the App accesses

Through TikTok's OAuth login, the App requests only the access needed to publish videos to the developer's own TikTok account (for example, the video.publish scope). It does not request access to, read, or store data belonging to other TikTok users, such as follower lists, other accounts' content, or personal profile information beyond what is needed to authenticate the developer's own account.

3. Data storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the developer's own machine and are used only to authorize publishing requests made by the developer. They are never transmitted to, or shared with, any third party.

4. Video content

Video files published through the App are the developer's own original content and are sent directly to TikTok's Content Posting API for publishing. The App does not retain copies on any server, because it does not operate a server — it runs locally.

5. No third-party sharing

No data handled by the App is sold, rented, or shared with any third party for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" page in account settings, which immediately invalidates any tokens issued to the App.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time by revising this page.
